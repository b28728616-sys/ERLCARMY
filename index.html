Tier 3

ODA (Green Berets)

ODA teams specialize in unconventional warfare, partner-force support, training, advising, and complex missions requiring judgment and discretion.

Rank Structure

  • Officer leadership: Captain (CPT) ODA commander; First Lieutenant (1LT) assistant ODA commander
  • Warrant expertise: Chief Warrant Officer 3-2 (CW3-CW2) technical and operational specialists
  • Senior enlisted: Sergeant First Class (SFC) team sergeant; Master Sergeant (MSG) senior advisor
  • Team personnel: Staff Sergeant (SSG), Sergeant (SGT), Specialist (SPC), and junior enlisted support

Tier 2

Rangers

Rangers provide highly trained light-infantry capability for direct action, raids, reconnaissance, security, and rapid-response missions.

Rank Structure

  • Battalion leadership: Lieutenant Colonel (LTC) battalion commander; Command Sergeant Major (CSM) senior enlisted advisor
  • Company leadership: Captain (CPT) company commander; First Sergeant (1SG) company senior enlisted leader
  • Platoon leadership: First Lieutenant (1LT) or Second Lieutenant (2LT) platoon leader; Sergeant First Class (SFC) platoon sergeant
  • Squad leadership: Staff Sergeant (SSG), Sergeant (SGT), Specialist (SPC), and junior enlisted personnel

Tier 1

1st Special Forces Operational Detachment-Delta

Delta Force handles the most sensitive and demanding missions, including high-risk counterterrorism, hostage rescue, special reconnaissance, and precision action.

Rank Structure

  • Unit leadership: Colonel (COL) group commander; Lieutenant Colonel (LTC) squadron commander
  • Operational leadership: Major (MAJ) or Captain (CPT) troop commander; Command Sergeant Major (CSM) senior enlisted advisor
  • Technical leadership: Chief Warrant Officer 5-1 (CW5-CW1) specialists assigned to aviation, communications, intelligence, or logistics
  • Operator leadership: Sergeant Major (SGM), First Sergeant (1SG), Master Sergeant (MSG), and Sergeant First Class (SFC)
  • Operators and support: Staff Sergeant (SSG), Sergeant (SGT), Specialist (SPC), Private First Class (PFC), and Private (PV2/PVT)

Rules of Engagement

Special Forces ROE

All tiers follow the same rules of engagement as every other soldier operating in a hostile area. Complex missions do not remove the requirements for lawful orders, positive identification, necessity, proportionality, and command accountability.

  • Use force only when authorized, necessary, and proportionate to the threat.
  • Distinguish confirmed hostile action from suspicion or an unverified assumption.
  • When time permits, pause, communicate, de-escalate, and seek command guidance.
  • Document the facts, warnings, evidence, and proportionality of serious use-of-force decisions.
  • Expect independent review when an action is disputed; good intent does not replace a defensible justification.
  • No tier may conduct extrajudicial action, retaliatory force, or unauthorized targeting.
